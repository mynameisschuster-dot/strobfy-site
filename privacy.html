Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

STROBFY is developed and operated by Stefan Schuster. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the STROBFY mobile application and when you sign up for launch updates on strobfy.com.

1. Our privacy approach

STROBFY processes your photos, text, and creative settings locally on your device. STROBFY does not require an account and does not operate its own servers for storing or processing your content.

2. Photos and exports

STROBFY accesses only the photos you choose through Apple’s system photo picker. Your media is processed locally, is not uploaded to STROBFY servers, is not accessed by the developer, and is not used for advertising or analytics. You retain ownership and control of your content.

Generated videos are saved or shared only when you choose to export them. If you share an export through another app or service, that provider’s privacy policy applies.

3. Personal data

The STROBFY app does not require registration and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, or account details. The app does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics services. The only exception on the website is the optional launch newsletter described in section 4.

4. Launch newsletter on strobfy.com

On strobfy.com you can leave your email address to be notified when STROBFY is available on the App Store. Signing up is voluntary. We process your email address only to send you this notification and occasional STROBFY news, based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

We use a double opt-in: after signing up you receive an email asking you to confirm your address. Your address is only added to the list once you confirm.

The newsletter is sent via Buttondown (buttondown.com), a newsletter service based in the United States. Your email address, the time of sign-up and confirmation, and technical delivery data are stored with Buttondown. Buttondown may also record whether an email was opened or a link was clicked. Data may be processed in the United States; Buttondown’s own privacy policy applies to its processing.

You can withdraw your consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to support@strobfy.com. Your email address is then removed from the list.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. STROBFY does not receive or store your payment card or banking information. Apple may provide limited transaction information required to verify or restore a purchase and handles that information under its own privacy policies.

6. Local storage

STROBFY may store your app settings, purchase-entitlement status, current project content, and imported project media locally on your device. Deleting the app may remove local settings and project information. Non-consumable purchases can be restored through the App Store.

7. Data sharing

STROBFY does not sell, rent, or share your personal data. Content is transferred to another app or service only when you actively choose to export or share it using Apple’s system features. Newsletter email addresses are processed only by Buttondown as described in section 4.

8. Children’s privacy

STROBFY is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app can generate rapid flashing effects, children should use STROBFY only with appropriate adult supervision and after reviewing the photosensitivity warning inside the app.

9. Data security

Because projects and media are processed locally, STROBFY does not transmit them to its own servers. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and choosing where exported content is stored or shared.

10. Your choices

You control which media you select, which effects you apply, whether you export or share content, and whether you purchase additional features. You can remove local app data by deleting projects or uninstalling the app. You decide whether to sign up for the launch newsletter and can unsubscribe at any time.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Because the STROBFY app processes content only on your device and holds no personal data on any server, there is normally no stored app data for such a request to cover. For newsletter subscribers, a request covers the email address stored with Buttondown. If you contact the developer by email, that message is processed only to answer you. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when STROBFY’s features or legal requirements change. The latest version is always available inside the app and on this page.
